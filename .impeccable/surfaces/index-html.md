# Surface brief: index.html (Antigravity Ticketing landing)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: event organizer/committee deciding on a package. Action: pick a package and send a registration request (WhatsApp/email). Content: existing Indonesian copy, plans, modules, contacts. No customer logos or testimonials exist; none are fabricated. Stack: static HTML/CSS/JS, Plus Jakarta Sans pinned by user. Visual direction pinned by the user's reference image (B2B SaaS, sky gradient with clouds, soft white cards).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The ticketing flow itself is the hero. The page proves the product by letting the visitor register, get a ticket, scan it, and price a package, on one calm sky-to-white surface. Refuses the dark, dense "event platform" look and the equal-card feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: Daytime sky. Deep-to-pale blue gradient ground with slow-drifting soft clouds, white rounded surfaces (16-24px) with one soft shadow and no borders, one vivid blue action color, navy ink, Plus Jakarta Sans only. Product UI mockups are the imagery. Recognizable with copy removed: blue sky, clouds, floating white product frames fading into white.

STORY: Organizer understands in seconds that this issues QR e-tickets and runs gate check-in, believes it by operating the demo, then picks a package by capacity and sends a request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating pill nav (logo left, five section links, CMS Organizer + primary action right). Centered white H1 on the deepest sky, one-line sub, two buttons (Estimasi Biaya Paket primary, Lihat Alur Kerja secondary). Below, a browser-framed attendance dashboard with an e-ticket card overlapping its edge, the bottom of the frame fading into white. Clouds drift behind the frame.

FORM: user-pinned reference (centered SaaS landing with bento features, pricing on sky, closing CTA on sky); seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
