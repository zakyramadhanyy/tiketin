# Surface brief: cms.html (Antigravity CMS, Superadmin & Event Organizer overview)

Mode: Operate. User: superadmin / event organizer at a desk in daylight, checking registration, ticket and check-in numbers and clearing the approval queue. Task: scan KPIs, spot trends, approve and send tickets, find an attendee fast. Layout is pinned by the user's reference: collapsible left sidebar with workspace (event) switcher, header with page title + search + notifications, a KPI strip, two chart cards, then tabs (Kanban / Table / Timeline) over a status board. Visual world is inherited from the landing page (DESIGN.md): sky wash ground, white rounded surfaces with soft blue shadow, one action blue, Plus Jakarta Sans. Data is demo and labelled "Data contoh"; the real CMS data was not accessible (login not performed by Claude).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing's daytime sky becomes a working console: a white app frame floating on a sky wash, calm tinted canvas, white data surfaces, one blue for action. Refuses a dark admin skin and a grid of identical stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: Sky-wash page ground, white 24px app frame, white sidebar, blue-tint canvas, white 16px cards with the landing's soft blue shadow, blue-ink active states, pill buttons, 12px inputs, status chips (warn/blue/neutral/ok). Charts in blue and sky, no other hues.

STORY: Organizer sees the state of the event in one glance, acts on the queue in place (approve, send ticket, check-in), and can export what is on screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sidebar (event switcher, nav, active events, portals, user) + header (title, search with shortcut hint, bell, primary action) + event context row with period control + one joined KPI strip of four figures with deltas; first chart card starts below.

FORM: pinned by the user's reference layout; seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
