# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user's choice): a single index.html with vanilla CSS and JS, no build step.

## Users

Event organizers and committees (conferences, summits, concerts) running events of roughly 100 to 10,000+ attendees. They need to register attendees, issue tickets, and control entry at the gate. Buyers decide on capacity and budget.

## Product Purpose

Antigravity Ticketing Platform manages event tickets end to end: custom registration forms, unique QR e-tickets sent by email after approval, smartphone gate scanning, and attendance reports exportable to Excel. Success is an organizer choosing a package and submitting a registration request.

## Positioning

Ticketing built for Indonesian events: no dedicated scanner hardware (staff scan with phones), anti-forgery QR tickets (HMAC-SHA256 signing), plus optional interactive audience modules (lucky draw, photo wall, stage quiz, photo quest) that most ticketing tools do not bundle.

## Operating Context

Flow: attendee fills a registration form, organizer approves, QR e-ticket is emailed, gate staff scan with a phone camera, attendance is exported to .xlsx. Organizers manage events in a separate CMS Organizer portal. Pricing is in Rupiah, by attendee capacity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language of copy: Indonesian (existing site copy is Indonesian).
- Sections on the current site: Ringkasan Platform, Alur Registrasi & Check-In, Fitur & Keandalan, Estimasi Biaya, Modul Interaktif, Kontak & Layanan.
- Plans: Starter Rp 500.000 (up to 500 attendees, 1 event); Professional Rp 1.500.000 (up to 2,500 attendees, 5 events, most popular); Enterprise Rp 3.500.000 (up to 10,000 attendees, 999 events, on-site support, SLA 99.98%).
- Capacity slider recommends a package from attendee count.
- Interactive modules: Lucky Draw, Interactive Photo Wall, Stage Quiz, Photo Quest.
- The current CMS Organizer link points at a localhost address; the real destination is undecided and must not be invented.

Brand name stays "Antigravity" (Ticketing Platform), company PT Antigravity Karya Global. Existing copy, prices, and contact details are kept as on the current site.

## Product Principles

- Show the real flow (register, approve, ticket, scan, export) rather than describe it abstractly.
- Pricing is transparent and tied to attendee count.
- Reliability and anti-forgery claims are stated only as the product states them.
- Organizer success first: every section should move toward choosing a package.
